ADA compliance FAQ for US ecommerce

Is my US Shopify store ADA compliant?
Not automatically. Your live Shopify theme, apps, product media, custom components, navigation, forms, and checkout-adjacent flows still need to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA expectations used in US ADA reviews.
How much does a US ADA compliance lawsuit cost?
One US ADA website claim can combine settlement pressure, attorney fees, emergency remediation, and internal distraction. For most online stores, proactive scanning and remediation costs less than reacting after a demand letter.
What standard matters in the United States?
In the US, WCAG 2.2 Level AA is the de facto standard for ADA compliance reviews, settlement language, and accessibility audit programs for ecommerce sites.
