ADA Compliance for US Ecommerce Platforms

These guides are written for US online stores, not generic global accessibility content. Under ADA Title III, public-facing ecommerce sites serving US customers are expected to be accessible. In the US, WCAG 2.2 Level AA is the de facto standard used in audits, settlements, and remediation programs.

California accounts for roughly 40% of tracked US ADA web lawsuits. New York and Florida follow. If your store sells nationally, you already have US exposure even if your team is based elsewhere.

US ecommerce lawsuit proof points

  • 8,667 ADA lawsuits were filed in the US in 2025.
  • 77% targeted ecommerce.
  • 32% of tracked cases involved Shopify stores.
  • Fashion Nova paid $5.15 million.
